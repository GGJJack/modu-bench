# C-Eval

## 개요
C-Eval is a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite designed to assess advanced knowledge and reasoning abilities of foundation models in a Chinese context. The benchmark comprises 13948 multiple-choice questions across four difficulty levels: middle school, high school, college, and professional. [출처: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.08322]

## 평가 방법
The questions span 52 diverse disciplines, ranging from humanities to science and engineering. This broad coverage allows for a comprehensive assessment of a model's capabilities across various subjects and educational levels, providing a detailed understanding of its strengths and weaknesses. [출처: https://github.com/SJTU-LIT/ceval]

## 점수 해석 및 C-Eval Hard
C-Eval is accompanied by C-Eval Hard, a subset of very challenging subjects in C-Eval that requires advanced reasoning abilities to solve. The evaluation of advanced LLMs indicates that only highly capable models like GPT-4 achieve high accuracy, highlighting significant room for improvement for current models. [출처: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.08322]
